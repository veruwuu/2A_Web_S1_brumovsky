Carter De Filippis (born August 17, 2001), known professionally as ThxSoMch, is a Canadian singer-songwriter. From Toronto, he started his career when he released his first single, "Runaway (Move Quick)" in September 2022. Gaining attention he released his biggest single "Spit in My Face!" in November 2022; as of July 2025, has garnered over 690 million streams on Spotify and charted internationally.

His large music genre consists of emo-pop, post-punk and electronic pop.

ThxSoMch often writes about his own experiences, struggles and heartbreak. From being cheated on, through drugs and obsessive feelings, to feelings of helplessness and loneliness.

Besides his music, Thx is also liked for his personality and genuine love for his fans. He goes above and beyond to make every fan feel seen, answering fan messages on social media and in his Discord server. He is a big advocate for mental health.
