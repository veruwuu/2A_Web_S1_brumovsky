“SPIT IN MY FACE!” is a post-punk and alternative rap track, describing the emotions of love, drug influence and an event that happened in his life while at work. The track was first previewed on TikTok as a snippet in October 2022 and blew up shortly after, with thousands of videos using the song.

The song follows ThxSoMch falling deep into a toxic relationship, to the point were he’d bleed for her, but with no love sent back. It’s a battle between the pair, with Thx realizing how toxic the relationship is, yet doesn’t want to be left alone.
