“SPIT IN MY FACE!” is a post-punk and alternative rap track, describing the emotions of love, drug influence and an event that happened in his life while at work. The track was first previewed on TikTok as a snippet in October 2022 and blew up shortly after, with thousands of videos using the song.

The song follows ThxSoMch falling deep into a toxic relationship, to the point were he’d bleed for her, but with no love sent back. It’s a battle between the pair, with Thx realizing how toxic the relationship is, yet doesn’t want to be left alone.

The song “Hate” is about the feeling of self-awareness that is executive to the freedom of the individual. It describes how the singer felt deceived and betrayed, and how his emotions became more vivid and intense when he realized it.

The performer repeats the phrase “I hate you” to convey his hatred and disgust. He also says that this person cannot trust him, and he is not going to waste his time on them.

The recurring line ‘This drum gon’ beat, but not for long,’ echoes the transient nature of life and the finite beats of our existence. It is an admission that while our presence is powerful and our actions impactful, they are ultimately ephemeral. ThxSoMch crafts a haunting reminder of mortality and the urgency it implicates.

The protagonist pushes forward, unyielding, with an attitude of stoic indifference symbolized by the phrase ‘keep it tucked.’
